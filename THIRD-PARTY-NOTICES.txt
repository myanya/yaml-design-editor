YAML Design Editor — third-party software

YAML Design Editor itself is distributed under the MIT license in LICENSE.
Third-party libraries retain their own licenses and copyright notices.

The binary distribution includes:
- licenses/dependencies/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt: resolved library versions,
  license declarations and embedded notices, generated from runtimeClasspath.
- licenses/SOURCES.txt: corresponding source artifact locations by version.
- licenses/cica/: Cica font license and copyright notices.
- licenses/upstream/: supplemental notices from the matching upstream releases.
- runtime/legal/: the bundled Java runtime's license and legal notices.

Eclipse ELK and Xtext are distributed under EPL-2.0; the bundled EMF 2.12
components are distributed under EPL-1.0. Their library binaries are unmodified.
Their corresponding source archives are freely available at the versioned
Maven Central URLs in SOURCES.txt. The original about.html, license and notice
files remain in each JAR. These libraries are not relicensed under MIT.

JNA offers Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1-or-later. This distribution uses the
Apache-2.0 option; the original dual-license declaration is retained.

Skiko/Skia includes native image and text libraries; supplemental notices
for the Windows build are in licenses/upstream/Skia-*.txt.
This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.

The JDK runtime and native libraries may include additional third-party
components. Their bundled legal notices remain applicable. When rebuilding
or redistributing, regenerate the reports for the actual target platform and
review any missing license information before publishing the distribution.
